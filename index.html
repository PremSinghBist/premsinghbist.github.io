About Me

I am Prem Singh Bist, PhD, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and computational biology, with an emphasis on developing predictive and generative models for biological sequences and therapeutic discovery.

My current work focuses on antimicrobial peptide activity prediction and computational peptide design. I investigate protein language models, deep learning, physicochemical representations, semi-supervised learning, interpretable modeling, and uncertainty-aware prediction to support more reliable computational approaches to peptide discovery.

My earlier research examined viral evolution and immune escape, including SARS-CoV-2 and influenza. I hold a PhD in Engineering from Jeonbuk National University, South Korea, and bring more than a decade of software development and technology leadership experience to research software and end-to-end AI workflows.

I aim to connect rigorous machine learning methods with meaningful challenges in biological discovery, antimicrobial resistance, and therapeutic development.
